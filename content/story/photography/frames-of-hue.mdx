I loaded a single roll of 36 exposures and promised myself that was all I would shoot. With film there is no preview, so you look at the street instead of the back of the camera.

## Rules for the roll

Limits make you pay attention. I kept the same few habits all morning.

![A placeholder image of a street at midday](/images/story/photography/frames-of-hue/mid.jpg)

- One lens, no zoom
- Wait for someone to walk into the frame
- Never shoot the same scene twice

When the roll finished, I had no idea what I had. That is half the fun.
