I took the morning train from Da Nang with a bag and no schedule. The ride is only a few hours, but the line hugs the coast and every window is a postcard.

## Eating my way through town

Huế is a city you walk with your stomach. I started each day with a bowl of bún bò and ended it with sweet soup by the river.

![A bowl of noodles on a plastic stool](/images/story/travel/hue-food-and-trains/gallery-2.jpg)

- Bún bò Huế for breakfast
- Cơm hến at lunch
- Chè by the river at night

## What I took home

Slow travel gives you time to notice details, the same way a good interface does.
