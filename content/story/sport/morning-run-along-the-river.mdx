I started running to clear my head after long days at the screen. Now it is the part of the week I protect the most.

## The route

The loop follows the river from the bridge to the old citadel wall and back. It is flat, quiet at 5:30 and just long enough to feel like an effort.

![Running shoes on the riverside steps](/images/story/sport/morning-run-along-the-river/gallery-2.jpg)

- Three runs a week, never two days in a row
- Easy pace, so I can still breathe through my nose
- Water and a banana waiting at home

## What it teaches me

Some mornings every step is slow. I keep going anyway, and that habit carries over to hard bugs and long projects.
